## Notes (from PLAN.md)

Branch `grid`. `draw_grid` in `kipp.f90` (after the convection hatch, under the surface line): per visible model one zig-zag `giza_line` through its stored cell faces (`xmcoord`/`rncoord`, bisected to the visible y range) plus a vertical line at each strip edge (`xedge`, the midpoints the bands already use), spanning the inner boundary to the taller neighbour's surface. Strips under a device pixel (`giza_get_viewport` in pixels) merge into the next drawn one and faces within a pixel of the last kept are skipped. `mesaload` exports `cnv_loaded`; with it set, `grid on` prints once that a KEPLER `.cnv` stores a reduced grid. Full grid (`ncoord = nz`): `.kipp`, MESA dirs, seq, atse-written `.cnv`; keppy `convdata.cnv` holds 85-332 points per model with median neighbouring dm ratio ~2.3 (max 1e9), so not every KEPLER zone (inferred from the file, `.scratch/cnv_coord_usage.py`).
